FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

Can't find your answer? Call (303) 555-0142 and a real roofer will pick up.

Storm just hit? Document damage with photos, don't climb up, and call us. Emergency tarping is free with any repair.
How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential re-roofs are finished in 1–2 days. Larger or steep roofs, tile and slate can take 3–5 days. We give you a firm schedule before work starts.

Will my insurance cover storm damage?

Usually, yes. Hail and wind damage are covered by most homeowner policies. We provide a HAAG-certified inspection report and meet your adjuster on-site to make the claim straightforward.

How do I know if I need a repair or full replacement?

If damage covers under 30% of the roof and the shingles are less than 15 years old, a repair usually makes sense. Beyond that, replacement is more cost-effective. Our inspection tells you honestly which one applies.

What does the 25-year warranty cover?

Our workmanship warranty covers any installation defect for 25 years, transferable once to a new owner. Manufacturer material warranties (up to lifetime) are registered on your behalf.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We partner with two lenders to offer 0% APR for 18 months on approved credit, or longer terms up to 12 years. Applications take about five minutes.
